## What You're Looking At

**Dana Point PULSE** is a live tourism analytics intelligence platform that combines STR hotel data, Datafy geolocation visitor economy data, CoStar market intelligence, and social media performance into a single decision-support system. ## 5 Key Insights to Call Out

### 1. Q3 Compression Is Already Maxed Out
Dana Point hit **34 compression days above 80% occupancy in Q3 2025** (15 days above 90%). Q1 had only 8 compression days. Hotels are full in summer. The growth opportunity is the shoulder season, not more summer volume.

> **Demo tip:** Go to Forward Outlook tab. Show the compression quarter-by-quarter chart. The Q3 vs Q1 gap is visual and immediate.

---

### 2. The Campaign Timing Signal (Hidden Risk)
This is a cross-dataset insight invisible in either STR or Datafy alone. Search channel drives the most attributed trips (204), but has the lowest conversion rate (0.61%). If Q3 campaign spend is concentrated in August, it generates marginal stays because hotels are already full. **Shifting 20-30% of Q3 campaign spend to Q1-Q2 shoulder months could generate 2-3x more incremental room nights per marketing dollar.**

> **Demo tip:** Go to Forward Outlook. The "Campaign Timing Signal" card is the orange flip card. Click it to reveal the full insight.

---

### 3. Out-of-State Visitors Are the High-Value Segment
61% of visitor days are out-of-state. Out-of-state visitors generate **60% of total tourism spending** despite being 61% of trips. Top feeder markets: Los Angeles (18.7%), San Diego (8.1%), Phoenix (7.3% — $298 avg spend), Las Vegas (3.6% — $378 avg spend). Fly markets (Phoenix, Las Vegas) spend 45-85% more per trip than drive markets.

> **Demo tip:** Go to Feeder Markets tab. The DMA breakdown chart shows volume vs. spend efficiency side by side.

---

### 4. Ohana Fest ROI Is Documented and Massive
Ohana Fest generates **$18.4M in destination spend, 3.2x economic multiplier, +$139 ADR lift**, with 68% out-of-state attendees. Media campaign attribution: 18.6M impressions, 2,472 attributable trips, $312K estimated economic impact. This is the clearest event ROI case study in the calendar.

> **Demo tip:** Go to Event Impact tab. The Ohana Fest metrics panel and ADR lift chart are the lead story.

---

### 5. Social Is Punching Above Its Weight
124,926 combined followers across IG (24,589), Facebook (99,794), and TikTok (543). Instagram engagement rate is **8.6%** — well above the 1-3% industry benchmark. Social reach is a leading demand indicator that shows up in STR data 6-8 weeks later.

> **Demo tip:** Go to Market Intelligence tab. Show the social channel performance panel. 8.6% IG engagement is a talking point.
